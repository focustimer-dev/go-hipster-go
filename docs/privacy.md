# Go, Hipster, Go! Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Go, Hipster, Go! plugin in ChatGPT.

The publisher does not receive or retain your ChatGPT prompts, playlist details, music account information, or plugin usage logs from the plugin. ChatGPT processes your playlist brief and any references you provide. When you request a save, ChatGPT may send the playlist title, description, and selected tracks to the authorized music service. OpenAI and the music service handle that information under their own privacy policies and settings.

If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. We use that information to respond and troubleshoot your request.

For privacy questions or to request deletion of support correspondence, email [gohipstergosupport@proton.me](mailto:gohipstergosupport@proton.me).
