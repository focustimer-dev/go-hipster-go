# Head-to-head: four-axis curation versus musical-method curation

**Research / playlist drafts only. Neither playlist was saved.** This is a **human-constructed, source-researched comparison**, not an autonomous agent A/B run, blind listening result or verified Spotify playlist.

## Fixed brief and controls

> Twelve real, musically coherent recordings moving from hypnotic guitar grooves to unusual vocals, repetition and studio experimentation.

Each approach uses **12 different artists**, **six identical anchor recordings**, no NWW source-membership score, and the same source-verification standard. The six anchors are Phương Tâm, Los Mirlos, Hailu Mergia & Walias Band, Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan & Party, Magma and This Heat. All 18 unique recordings have a label, artist, archive or public catalog source in [the dataset](competing-playlists.json). **These sources verify artist/title listings, not necessarily a particular Spotify recording ID or local market availability.**

The playlist drafts are intentionally useful to listen to, not pretense about passing a model performance test.

**Curator reasoning:** Saigon 1960s electric vocals → Khmer-language pop → Peruvian electric cumbia → Anatolian psych/funk → Zambian garage rock → Ethio-jazz → extended qawwali ensemble → zeuhl vocal/rock suite → This Heat's loop experiment.

**Strength:** geographic/history variety, many concise guitar/funk songs, familiar entry points into difficult material.

**Weakness:** transitions 9→10→11→12 are connected mainly through the broad ideas of repetition, intensity and oddity; the underlying *function* changes fast and can feel arbitrary. Zambian rock is represented twice; no independent listener has verified the sequence's flow.

## B — Rhythm, Taken Apart (method/operation/role)

1. **Phương Tâm — [Đêm Huyền Diệu (Magical Night)](https://sublime-frequencies.bandcamp.com/album/magical-nights-saigon-surf-twist-soul-1964-1966)**
2. **Los Mirlos — [La Danza de los Mirlos](https://barbes.bandcamp.com/track/la-danza-de-los-mirlos)**
3. **Etran de L'Aïr — [Toubouk Ine Chihoussay (Agadez studio)](https://etrandelair.bandcamp.com/track/toubouk-ine-chihoussay-2)**
4. **Hailu Mergia & Walias Band — [Tche Belew](https://hailumergia.bandcamp.com/track/tche-belew)**
5. **Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan & Party — [Allah, Mohammed, Char, Yaar](https://realworldrecords.com/releases/shahen-shah/)**
6. **Magma — [Da Zeuhl Wortz Mekanïk (1973 album sequence)](https://open.spotify.com/embed/album/6MDeMi4c1Zo1KRKtCgZpsb)**
7. **Senyawa — [Alkisah I (2021 studio recording)](https://senyawaofficial.bandcamp.com/track/alkisah-i-5)**
8. **This Heat — [24 Track Loop](https://thisheat.bandcamp.com/track/24-track-loop)**
9. **DJ Screw & Point Blank — [My Mind Went Blank (Screwed)](https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/7zfLS7MJmy8zmj1OL7s6bk)**
10. **J Dilla — [Workinonit (Donuts, 2006)](https://dilla.bandcamp.com/track/workinonit-2)**
11. **Pierre Henry — [Variations pour une porte et un soupir: 2. Balancement 1 (Galaxie Pierre Henry)](https://open.spotify.com/album/3Lnkr3mGrY3vYUlB7w7fFr)**
12. **KMRU — [Klang (Peel, 2020)](https://kmru.bandcamp.com/album/peel)**

**Curator reasoning by transition (source-based hypotheses, not listened-to claims):**
1. Phương Tâm → Los Mirlos: electric melodic hook toward repeated guitar pulse.
2. Los Mirlos → Etran de L'Aïr: repeated guitar figure stays central, but moves to a distinct Agadez performance tradition.
3. Etran → Hailu Mergia: steady ensemble propulsion redirects from guitar to keyboard/horn-led arrangement.
4. Hailu → Nusrat: build intensity via recurring phrases, from instrumental groove to a **specifically devotional** lead/chorus performance (not an equivalent purpose).
5. Nusrat → Magma: *formal* ensemble-vocal accumulation, with clear religious/constructed-rock differences and no implied influence.
6. Magma → Senyawa: forceful stylized singing and elaborate instrumental/performance methods; Indonesian narrative is not a mystical stand-in for zeuhl.
7. Senyawa → This Heat: intentionally awkward shift from handmade instruments/live voice to tape-based reconstruction; method-level claim needs listening.
8. This Heat → DJ Screw: rhythmic recordings transformed by technology and time; no invented cultural or causal influence.
9. DJ Screw → J Dilla: rhythmic/sampling operation persists, with different rap histories and playback choices.
10. J Dilla → Pierre Henry: hard cut from beat collage to object/breath recordings organized into a short fixed-media movement; likely the **highest transition risk**.
11. Pierre Henry → KMRU: recorded sound and environmental/electronic composition; clear method family, different scale and affect.

**Strength:** explains why apparently remote genres could connect *without pretending they share origin or genre*. Etran de L'Aïr is rooted in **Niger**, Senyawa in **Indonesia**, and KMRU in **Kenya**; the regional breadth comes from defendable sonic connections, not a country quota.

**Weakness:** it drifts from grooves toward experimental composition in its final minutes. The method overlay is doing intellectual work, but whether a listener enjoys that ending is untested. B draws **3 NWW-pilot artists** (Magma, This Heat, Pierre Henry) versus A's **2** (Magma, This Heat), so there is a small selection-bias signal even without an explicit membership bonus.

## What would establish utility?

1. Listen to both sets in order, not to isolated standout tracks. Flag each transition as coherent, deliberate contrast, or unjustified.
2. For the same 18 artist/title source-supported records, resolve **exact recording version and provider ID** on the chosen streaming service. The different editions of Ros Serey Sothea, Magma and Henry warrant extra care.
3. Ask for a **blind preference** from at least one listener who doesn't know which list is 'enhanced'. The method list must be judged as music, not on the elegance of its explanation.
4. Challenge B with a revised sequence that **keeps the strong bridges but avoids ending with two sound-art entries**. Improvements in narrative alone do not justify production.
5. Only after a demonstrated advantage add a small method/function heuristic to the production skill. No NWW score, no blanket artist import, and preserve the DeBarge protocol.
