### Why these three changes?

### 4. Erkin Koray → Etran de L'Aïr

**Before:** Erkin Koray — [Cemalim](https://music.apple.com/us/song/1701826019). **Now:** Etran de L'Aïr — [Toubouk Ine Chihoussay (Agadez studio)](https://etrandelair.bandcamp.com/track/toubouk-ine-chihoussay-2).

**Material:** repeating electric-guitar figures. **Operation:** move cycling melodic riffs from Peruvian cumbia toward Saharan ensemble propulsion. **Purpose in the sequence:** retain a recognizable groove while crossing a large stylistic/geographic distance.

- **Incoming:** Los Mirlos → Etran de L'Aïr
- **Outgoing:** Etran de L'Aïr → Mustafa Özkent
- **Hypothesized advantage:** a meaningful long-distance guitar bridge rather than a second guitar-psych reference
- **Possible regression:** a contemporary Niger recording between older Peruvian and Turkish studio sounds can reveal a production-era jump
- **Evidence:** [source](https://etrandelair.bandcamp.com/track/toubouk-ine-chihoussay-2) · [source](https://barbes.bandcamp.com/track/la-danza-de-los-mirlos)
- **Judgment:** plausible, unheard sequence. This is *not* a verified audible transition.

### 7. Ngozi Family → Ebo Taylor

**Before:** Ngozi Family — [Hi Babe](https://ngozifamily.bandcamp.com/album/day-of-judgment). **Now:** Ebo Taylor — [Heaven](https://ebotaylor.bandcamp.com/track/heaven-1).

**Material:** electric guitars, live funk rhythm, arranged horns. **Operation:** move a heavy fuzz-rock groove toward layered Ghanaian highlife/funk. **Purpose in the sequence:** keep the listener dancing while preparing Hailu Mergia's instrumental ensemble sound.

- **Incoming:** WITCH → Ebo Taylor
- **Outgoing:** Ebo Taylor → Hailu Mergia & Walias Band
- **Hypothesized advantage:** removes back-to-back Zamrock artists and bridges toward Ethio-jazz via funk arrangement
- **Possible regression:** 'Heaven' is a longer cut; transition depends on actual dynamics, which have not been listened to
- **Evidence:** [source](https://ebotaylor.bandcamp.com/track/heaven-1) · [source](https://hailumergia.bandcamp.com/track/tche-belew)
- **Judgment:** plausible, unheard sequence. This is *not* a verified audible transition.

### 12. This Heat → This Heat

**Before:** This Heat — [24 Track Loop](https://thisheat.bandcamp.com/track/24-track-loop). **Now:** This Heat — [Health and Efficiency (1980 studio single; artist remaster)](https://thisheat.bandcamp.com/track/health-and-efficiency-2).

**Material:** experimental rock group, instrumental momentum and an opening vocal passage. **Operation:** replace primarily tape-loop demonstration with a gradually intensifying live-band composition. **Purpose in the sequence:** resolve Magma's ensemble force in a driving finale without switching to pure sound art.

- **Incoming:** Magma → This Heat
- **Outgoing:** end
- **Hypothesized advantage:** retains This Heat eccentricity but finishes on an energetic band track, not a tape-loop exercise
- **Possible regression:** still an eight-minute long-form track, not a guaranteed breezy closer
- **Evidence:** [source](https://thisheat.bandcamp.com/track/health-and-efficiency-2) · [source](https://www.magmamusic.org/en/discography/mekanik-destruktiw-kommandoh-2/)
- **Judgment:** source-supported, listening untested. This is *not* a verified audible transition.

## Decision framework after actual listening

- **Promote only an abstract method/function prompt:** If at least two changed regions feel better to a listener *and* the whole set isn't worse, trial a brief candidate-discovery step in research. This is a **heuristic gate**, not statistically validated performance. Keep the existing four axes and require exact track source evidence.
- **Revise C:** If Etran's production-era jump is distracting, find another verified cycling-guitar recording from the wider universe; the finding survives even if this song doesn't.
- **Reject/pause:** If A is preferred, retain the current skill and learn from *why* specific transitions failed. Never promote NWW membership as a relevance boost.
- **Resolve for streaming separately:** At least one candidate (e.g., Magma's track within a seven-part suite) can have remaster and edition ambiguity; verify the exact intended artist/title/version in the target provider before any publication.
