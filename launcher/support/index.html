Before you email

Open the Manual for skill-installation instructions, selected AI setup, and how to copy, review and publish with supported external tools. The app creates a discovery request; it does not automatically save a playlist or install an AI skill for you.

For a problem, tell us the Android version, device and OS, which AI/music service you selected, what you attempted, and what happened. A screenshot is useful when it does not expose private data.

Common questions

Where are my saved prompts?

Open Your Crate in the Android app. Saved requests and recent inspirations are kept locally on that installation, not synchronized to other devices. Clearing Android app data or uninstalling removes local content. Lab, RC and production installs have separate storage.

Why did an AI skill not update?

Opening a repository page or downloading a ZIP does not install a skill. Follow the Manual's installation steps and verify the installed version inside the selected AI service. The app can check GitHub release information; it cannot inspect or upgrade a saved Gemini skill by itself.

Why was a playlist not saved?

The AI/music service must support authorized playlist creation and saving. Go, Hipster, Go! only prepares the request; verify what the external AI actually completed before concluding it was saved.

How do I make a privacy inquiry?

Read the Privacy Policy and email the support address above for data-handling questions, corrections or deletion requests concerning information we control.

Reporting an issue

Include the exact error message and steps to reproduce. Please do not include passwords, API keys, access tokens or identifying playlist/account details in email or screenshots.

Support email is handled separately from any external AI or music account; we cannot reset third-party credentials or delete data stored exclusively by those services.
