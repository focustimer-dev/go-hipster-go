# Flexible coherence initial trial — 28 October 2026

Candidate: PR #45. Baseline: 818e5ab. Three same-prompt text-only curation probes per version; 12 tracks each. All six outputs had 12 distinct lead artists. Neither run auditioned audio or published anything.

## What the probe establishes

Both versions produced concrete sets across the regional mashup, Can-alternatives and worldwide briefs. The candidate identified specific uncertain handoffs (Stereolab → Circle; Siti Muharam → Maurice Louca) rather than establishing that they sounded good. A flexible thesis did not prevent artist diversity in this small sample. This is a feasibility observation, not a measured improvement or optimality claim.

## Comparison limits

The baseline used 39 web queries in 10 batches and primary artist/label catalogs. The candidate used 60 Spotify queries in six batches, one collection read and four web queries. Consequently verification access and effort were not matched: this is NOT a controlled efficacy A/B. The baseline also reported truncated regional reference reads; the candidate reread at least one truncated file. Catalog matches do not verify sonic transitions. Both versions included familiar reference artists. No conclusion about lower cost, better flow, robustness, Gemini compliance or wider discovery is supported.

## Quantifying flow next

Hold twelve verified recording identities fixed and compare two orders blind, with playback settings constant. For each of eleven handoffs collect natural / worthwhile surprise / disruptive labels; report disruptive count out of eleven, worthwhile-surprise count and overall A/B/tie separately. Assess monotony/discovery variety separately so bland uniformity cannot win by smoothness alone. Repeat across different briefs and fresh runs before promotion. Use full tracks or adequate ending-to-opening listening; BPM and key alone cannot establish flow. These listener labels have not yet been collected.
