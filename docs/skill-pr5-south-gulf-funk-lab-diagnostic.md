## Compilation/source concentration

**Four of twelve** songs — #3 Aaron Neville, #5 Chuck Carbo, #6 The Explosions and #7 Eddie Bo — appear on the **same** Soul Jazz Records `New Orleans Funk: Original Sound of Funk 1960–75` compilation. (They also have separate original or reissue contexts, some named in the playlist.) Eight of the twelve are from other album/single/soundtrack/anthology sources. This is markedly better *source diversity* than the **Central America Lab's 10/12 from two compilations**, including six from one Soundway volume. Different regions and source ecologies mean this is **diagnostic evidence**, not a causal proof that the optimization improved or worsened quality.

The Southern set includes a range of plausible scene connections (Miami, Memphis, New Orleans, Dallas, San Antonio, Houston, Atlanta) and some deeper record paths (Timothy McNealy, Mickey & the Soul Generation, the Kashmere Stage Band). A few well-known records (Isaac Hayes, Aaron Neville, the Bar-Kays) remain musically sensible; GO HARD shouldn't be equated with prohibiting famous artists.

## Conclusion and next action

This **reduces concern about a general loss of curation breadth** from the lean optimization: the Central America playlist was unusually compilation-led, while the Southern set draws on much wider recording sources. It does **not** erase the Central America finding or prove live discovery funnel behavior.

Recommended **small corrective emphasis**, not a long new ruleset:
1. Repair **version-level release attribution**: if an archival 45 appears only as a bonus on a later album reissue, name the reissue explicitly or credit the original single rather than the original LP.
2. For **Skip rough recordings**, require supporting transfer-specific evidence where available. A label reissue alone does not prove a clean master; preserve honesty about unauditioned tracks and do not silently waive the filter.
3. For GO HARD, prefer multiple genuinely independent research pathways when strong source material is available. Do not impose artist/label/compilation quotas or remove research leads.
4. Keep the original optimized architecture, the public 3.11.5 skill, and `release/skill-prod` unchanged. PR #252 stays draft. Still need a controlled evaluation of at least one **ordinary GO** and host-specific behavior before calling the release ready.
