Select real exact recordings, verify versions/story/mood/tempo where evidence exists, and report unknown transfer quality honestly. No manufactured search history or code-based RNG. Explicit text-only means absolutely no music-service write.
5. **Refresh and exceptions.** Supplied inspiration/seed overrides internal seeding; a supplied route wins its starting direction. No automatic seeding for de-cheese, setup, or a publication retry. Antarctica remains opt-in. DeBarge remains governed by the existing exact one-roll exception.
