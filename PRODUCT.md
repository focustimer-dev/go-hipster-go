# Go, Hipster, Go!
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
adaptive

## Users
Listeners seeking adventurous, verified deep-cut playlists, using the mobile PWA or Android Lab. The primary acceptance device is an Android phone.

## Product Purpose
Prepare a concise curation request for a selected AI and music service. A successful handoff copies the current request and lets the listener paste and send it in the AI.

## Operating Context
Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript PWA hosted in a Cloudflare Worker, shared with an offline Android WebView shell. Android adds native handoffs, a request shelf, three recent inspirations, public HTTPS link previews and deliberate skill-update checks. The installed curation skill owns music research, verification and publication reconciliation. The launcher is not a music player or an authenticated streaming client.

## Capabilities and Constraints
AI choices: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and manual Other AI. Music choices: Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music; capability differences must remain truthful. Optional tempo, eight mood presets (up to two combined), interactive regional atlas, rotating crate sparks. One-finger map pan, two-finger pinch zoom, tap to select. Clipboard recovery, manual receipt and conditional AI handoff. Saved local preferences and a reversible first-visit URL override. No invented tracks, connections, publication success or music-history access.

## Brand Commitments
Go, Hipster, Go! name. Begrudgingly helpful record-store-clerk voice: precise condescension, passive aggressive, never cruel or function blocking. The approved gig-poster identity, exact original portraits, Criminal domino mask, atlas footprint and Robinson geometry are locked. Preserve the approved +ART/TRY HARD indicators and metal recording-preference lever. The visual specification is launcher/editions/gig-poster/DESIGN.md.

## Product Principles
- Preserve the clear copy, paste and send workflow.
- Make selected preferences and the next action obvious.
- Keep personality out of literal failure and recovery instructions.
- Mobile density must improve actual use without requiring additional disclosure taps.
- Confirmed setup moves into Preferences on subsequent Android launches.
- Antarctica requires explicit selection and stays out of general shuffle.
- Keep user-visible behavior stable during technical cleanup.

## Evidence on Hand
Canonical launcher sources and generated gig-poster pages; Android Lab wrapper and runtime asset manifest; automated core, bundle, native-policy, DOM, visual and atlas-performance checks. See android-lab/STABILIZATION.md for current verification and release acceptance. Existing copy and capability checks remain authoritative.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable mobile labels, keyboard access, visible focus, reduced motion, native text scaling, meaningful status and literal clipboard recovery. Do not rely on color alone.
