# Go, Hipster, Go!
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Listeners seeking adventurous, verified deep-cut playlists, including repeat mobile PWA use. The current user evaluates primarily on Android.

## Product Purpose
Prepare a concise curation request for a selected AI and music service. A successful handoff copies the current request and lets the listener paste and send it in the AI.

## Operating Context
Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript PWA hosted in a Cloudflare Worker. The installed curation skill owns music research, verification and publication reconciliation. The launcher is not a music player or an authenticated streaming client.

## Capabilities and Constraints
AI choices: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude. Music choices: Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music; capability differences must remain truthful. Optional tempo, five mood presets, interactive regional atlas, rotating crate sparks. One-finger map pan, two-finger pinch zoom, tap to select. Clipboard recovery, manual receipt and conditional AI handoff. Saved local preferences and a reversible first-visit URL override. No invented tracks, connections, publication success or music-history access.

## Brand Commitments
Go, Hipster, Go! name. Begrudgingly helpful record-store-clerk voice: precise condescension, passive aggressive, never cruel or function blocking. User requested two dramatically different visual alternatives grounded in their 2000s Hipster Visual Design Guide. Existing red star and mood portraits are available, not mandatory constraints for alternate compositions.

## Product Principles
- Preserve the clear copy, paste and send workflow.
- Make selected preferences and the next action obvious.
- Keep personality out of literal failure and recovery instructions.
- Mobile density must improve actual use without requiring additional disclosure taps.
- Alternate designs remain previews until approved; do not replace the live PWA.

## Evidence on Hand
Working launcher/index.html, launcher/assets/app.js, launcher/assets/hipster.css; 70-test suite; guide supplied through connected Drive. Existing copy and capability checks are authoritative product content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable mobile labels, keyboard access, visible focus, reduced motion, native text scaling, meaningful status and literal clipboard recovery. Do not rely on color alone.
