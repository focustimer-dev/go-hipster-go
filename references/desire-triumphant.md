# Desire and Triumphant

Read before selecting recordings for either mood, alone or combined. These are curation meanings; character artwork is presentation and never musical evidence. Existing regional, era-if-explicit, count, perceived pace, service, verification and sequencing rules remain authoritative. Mood does not automatically activate GO HARD, Antarctica, art or service writes.

## Desire: romantic love and longing

**Boundary:** every ordinary selection must express romantic love or longing with credible recording-specific lyrical, narrative or documented programmatic evidence. Rotate infatuation, mutual devotion, tenderness, courtship, seduction, separation, unrequited love, reunion, torch-song yearning and enduring attachment. Desire spans joyful and painful love; no automatic slow, sultry, English-language or jazz default. Respect the user's exclusions and romantic framing without assuming the listener's gender, sexuality or personal circumstances.

A sensual groove, breathy voice, artist reputation, romantic title, genre tag or model's memory alone does not establish the song's subject. Verify consequential meaning with reliable lyric/translation sources, artist/label commentary or reputable song-level discussion; do not invent translations or quote long lyrics. Do not turn protest, spiritual devotion, generalized aspiration or sexual swagger into romantic love without supported context. Instrumentals require credible documentation of a romantic program or subject for this exact work/recording; a pretty melody alone does not qualify. If the evidence cannot establish romantic fit, keep researching or report a shortfall instead of relabeling an uncertain track. Apply the existing deliberate special-case rules only when actually triggered.

Within that boundary, choose recordings for supported musical appeal and transitions, not twelve interchangeable love-ballad standards. Fast, strange, sparse, aggressive or rhythmically intricate love songs can qualify. Desire + another mood keeps the romantic-content boundary for every ordinary selected recording.

## Triumphant: earned arrival and expansive payoff

**Target:** a credible musical sense of victory, release, resilience or arrival—the hero reaching the victorious climax. Seek supported rising or opening arrangements, expansive harmonic gestures, soaring melodic contours, emphatic ensemble entrances, growing density, collective affirmation or a convincing large-scale payoff. Not all features are necessary; do not invent a crescendo, modulation, chord size, key or ending. Assess the actual recording/version using reliable source-supported description or genuinely available audio; state uncertainty and never claim to have listened without access.

Orchestrally significant scale is a strong direction, not an orchestra requirement or a soundtrack-only roster. Explore orchestral and cinematic music alongside gospel, soul, rock, electronic and regionally grounded ensemble traditions when credible musical evidence supports the effect. Avoid assuming every choir, brass section, major key, loud chorus, fast beat, heroic title or motivational lyric is triumphant. Do not infer a western harmonic template across traditions. A fantasy character does not imply medieval music, battle lyrics, militarism or a particular geography.

Slow triumph can be stately, monumental or gradually opening; fast triumph can be kinetic. A requested restrained pace still applies. Sad + Triumphant can be bittersweet survival; Angry + Triumphant can be defiance achieving release. Preserve a coherent sense of earned arrival without requiring every song to peak at maximum intensity. Sequence supported contrasts and quieter preparation around the defining mood; do not fabricate victory stories to justify a weak recording.

## Up to two selected moods

Treat two explicitly selected moods as one combined brief, with equal weight unless the listener specifies a priority. Do not silently split a twelve-song playlist into six songs for each mood. Seek the intersection, or a coherent emotional tension supported by the recordings. Preserve hard content boundaries: Desire always requires romance evidence; Criminal keeps its documented crime-narrative requirement, including when paired. If their intersection cannot credibly fill the requested count and regions, disclose the limitation and ask which constraint the listener wants to relax; do not invent content or silently alternate incompatible halves. For conflicting mood effects, explain a defensible interpretation rather than erasing one choice. More than two named moods in free text is conversational intent, not an error; clarify only if it changes selection materially.

## De-cheese and discovery routes

Check romantic interpretation and claimed musical payoff separately from overall song quality. Missing romance evidence makes Desire eligibility uncertain, not proven false. A disproven claimed crescendo may call for a note correction if other supported triumphant qualities survive. Do not replace an unsupported claim with another unsourced instrument, lyric or historical story. Keep the audit read-only and propose concrete repairs before asking the existing approval question.

Optional discovery routes and sparks guide candidate research within these mood requirements. They do not override them, supply facts or authorize broader regions/eras. Ordinary GO remains single-pass; explicit GO HARD retains its existing deeper funnel and evidence budget.
